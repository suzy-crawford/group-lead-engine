Freelancer To CEO Build guide

Your Facebook group lead engine

An app that finds your 20 busiest Facebook groups, writes a 6-week posting plan for each one, and tracks which posts bring in leads.

Build in: Base44 · new app · Build mode About 90 min 12 steps

What you'll walk away with

  • Your 20 most active groups: 10 in your niche, 10 local
  • A 6-week posting plan, written for every group
  • A Copy & open group button for posting in seconds
  • A tracker that shows which posts bring in leads

Before you start

  1. Sign up at apify.com. It's what searches Facebook. A full search costs about $0.40–$1.50 of credit.
  2. In Apify, go to Settings → API & Integrations and copy your Personal API token.
  3. In your new Base44 app, switch on backend functions, then add a secret named APIFY_TOKEN and paste your token in.

Where to paste it

  1. Open Base44 and go to your home dashboard.
  2. Start a new app (not the Agent tab).
  3. Check the chat is on Build mode, not Discuss.
  4. Paste Step 1 as one message and send it. Wait until it finishes.

Then: paste the next step into the same app's chat. Never start a new app between steps. One step at a time, and let each step finish before the next.

Step 2 of 12

Look and feel

Gives the whole app its look before any pages are built.

Apply this design system to the whole app now, and use it for every page I ask you to build after this. Keep all existing features, data and wording; this prompt only sets the look.

FEEL
Warm, editorial and premium. Think a good magazine, not a tech dashboard. Cream and black only: cream is the base, black is the anchor. Calm, lots of space, nothing flashy.

COLOURS (cream and black only; no other colours anywhere)
- Page background: cream #F7F2E8
- Cards, inputs and panels: light cream #FCF9F3 with a 1px border #E4DCCB
- Alternating sections, table header rows and tags: deeper cream #EFE8DA
- Main text and headings: black #141210
- Secondary text, labels and dates: warm grey #6E675C
- Primary buttons: black #141210 with cream text #F7F2E8; pressed state #2B2824
- Secondary buttons: transparent with a 1px #141210 border and black text
- Links: black, underlined
- Icons and list numbers: black
- Tip and callout panels: deeper cream #EFE8DA with a 3px black left border and black text
- Sidebar: black #141210 with cream text #F7F2E8, muted text #A39E93, hover and active item #24211D, dividers #2E2B26
- Status (done, warning, error): never use colour to show status. Use a short word plus a simple icon, e.g. a black "Posted" tag with a tick, or "Needs a result" in a callout panel.

TYPOGRAPHY
- Headings: Gloock (Google Fonts), regular weight 400, never bold. Page titles about 48–52px, section titles about 28px, with slightly tight letter spacing.
- Everything else: Inter, 400 for body, 600 for labels and buttons. Body 15–16px, line height 1.5.
- Small section labels: Inter 12px, uppercase, letter spacing 0.15em, colour #6E675C, with a short 40px x 2px black line before them.
- Sentence case for every heading, button and menu item.

SHAPE AND SPACING
- Corner radius: 12px on cards and panels, 8px on buttons and inputs, full pill only on small tags.
- Flat design: hairline borders instead of heavy shadows. Only menus and modals get a soft shadow.
- Generous spacing: 24–32px padding inside cards, 48–64px between sections.
- One clear primary button per screen.

LAYOUT
- Black sidebar on the left with cream text, the app name in Gloock at the top, and the active item on #24211D.
- Main area on cream with a big Gloock greeting, a one-line next step between hairlines, then black tool cards with cream text and simple cream line icons.
- Tables and lists use hairline rows (#E4DCCB), a light header row on #EFE8DA, and no zebra stripes.

DON'T
- No colours other than the creams, blacks and warm greys above: no purple, blue, green, red, pink or gradients.
- No pure white (#FFFFFF) or pure black (#000000) backgrounds.
- No logos or brand names other than the app's name.
- No glows, glassy blurred panels, sparkles or emoji in the interface.
- No bold Gloock and no all-caps headings.

HOW THIS APPLIES TO THIS APP
- Show "Group Lead Engine" as the app's name in the sidebar and the browser tab.
- The Home page uses the greeting layout: "Good morning / afternoon / evening, [first name]." by the time of day, the next step between hairlines, then black tool cards for Find groups, My groups, Post this week and Tracker.
- On phones (under 768px wide), the black sidebar becomes a black bottom tab bar with cream icons and labels, active item on #24211D. Page titles shrink to about 34–36px, and card padding to 20px. Keep 16px side margins and no sideways scrolling.
- The no-emoji rule is for the app's interface only. The text of Facebook posts keeps its emojis exactly as written.
- When I ask for a warning or highlight, use the callout panel with a short label. When I ask for something to show as done, use a black tag with a tick and a word, e.g. "Posted".
- On pages where every card has the same main action (e.g. "Copy & open group" on Post this week), that repeated button can be the black primary button on each card. Everything else on those cards is secondary.
- Charts on the Tracker: bars in black #141210, planned lines dashed in #6E675C, hairline gridlines #E4DCCB, numbers in Inter.

Step 4 of 12

Keyword generation

Adds an AI button that suggests the words your ideal clients search for.

Use the cream-and-black design system from Prompt 2 for everything in this step.

On the Find groups page, add a button "Suggest search keywords". Under it, show: "A full search uses about $0.40–$1.50 of your Apify credit and takes 20–30 minutes."

The button reads the user's Profile and uses the AI (InvokeLLM with a JSON schema) to suggest keywords, then shows them as editable chips in two lists, "Niche" and "Local". The user can delete, edit or add chips, up to 16 in total, then press "Start search". Pressing it creates a Search record with status "Keywords ready" and each keyword saved with run_status "queued".

Give the AI these rules exactly:

KEYWORD RULES
- Every keyword is 2–3 words. The search only keeps groups whose name or description contains EVERY word, so longer phrases return nothing.
- NICHE keywords (5–6):
  - Use the words the ideal client calls THEMSELVES, not words for what the marketer sells. E.g. for an ads manager for clinics: "clinic owners UK", "aesthetics business", "med spa owners".
  - Use "owners" or the job title owners use for themselves, not broad industry words. "Hospitality" and "catering" return jobs and equipment groups. For beauty, role words work best: "nail techs UK", "lash techs UK", "aesthetic practitioners UK", "hairdressers UK".
  - If client_reach is "Anywhere in my country", add the country, e.g. "restaurant owners UK". Without it, results come back from the Philippines, Dubai and India.
  - Use client_interests and other_hangouts to add 1–2 keywords for where these owners actually spend time.
  - Avoid ambiguous single words ("injectors" returned diesel fuel-injector groups).
- LOCAL keywords (6–8), a mix of business and community:
  - Business: "<town> business", "<town> small business", "<town> networking", "<region> business".
  - Community: "<town> mums" (UK) or "<town> moms" (US), "<town> parents", "<town> community", "<town> chat", "<neighbouring town> community".
  - Add 1–2 local keywords from client_interests if they fit, e.g. "<town> runners".
  - Include 1–2 neighbouring towns within about 30 minutes.
  - US: many towns share names, so also use the state, e.g. "Texas small business".
  - If the town is also an everyday word (Reading, Bath, Deal, March, Wells, Street, Ware, Sale), add the county ("Reading Berkshire") or use a neighbouring town instead.
- Never suggest keywords that would find groups for marketers, advertising, jobs or buy/sell.

Return JSON: { "niche": [string], "local": [string], "notes": string }. Show the notes above the chips.
Step 5 of 12

Apify search

Connects the app to Apify so it can find Facebook groups. Try one keyword first.

Use the cream-and-black design system from Prompt 2 for everything in this step.

Create two backend functions that call Apify. My Apify token is in the secret APIFY_TOKEN. Never send it to the browser or include it in any response.

Use the Apify actor "memo23/facebook-search-groups-scraper" (in API URLs: memo23~facebook-search-groups-scraper). Runs take 2–4 minutes, so never wait for a run inside a function: start runs, save their IDs, and let the page check back.

SHARED HELPER: freeSlots()
- Ask Apify how many runs are running on my account right now:
  GET https://api.apify.com/v2/actor-runs?status=RUNNING&limit=100 (header Authorization: Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN>)
- Return 5 minus that count, never below 0. (Apify's free plan allows 5 runs at once. This also counts runs I've started elsewhere in Apify.)

SHARED HELPER: startQueued(search)
- While freeSlots() > 0 and the Search has keywords with run_status "queued", start the next one:
  POST https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/memo23~facebook-search-groups-scraper/runs?memory=1024&maxTotalChargeUsd=0.3
  Header: Authorization: Bearer <APIFY_TOKEN>
  JSON body: {"searchQueries": ["<the one keyword>"], "maxGroups": 50, "requireKeywordMatch": true, "privacy": "any", "minMembers": 1000}
- Save run_id = data.id, dataset_id = data.defaultDatasetId, run_status = "running".
- If a start request fails or times out, mark that keyword "failed" with the error, and DON'T retry it automatically (it may have started anyway, and a retry could charge twice).
- Search status: "Searching" if any keyword is running, otherwise "Waiting".

FUNCTION 1: startGroupSearch({ searchId })
- The Search must have no more than 16 keywords.
- Set started_at = now, then run startQueued.

FUNCTION 2: checkGroupSearch({ searchId })
- For every keyword with run_status "running":
  GET https://api.apify.com/v2/actor-runs/<run_id> (same auth header)
  - If data.status is SUCCEEDED, fetch
    GET https://api.apify.com/v2/datasets/<dataset_id>/items?clean=true&fields=name,url,groupId,privacy,membersCount,postsLastDay,postsLastMonth,description,groupDescription,rules,searchQuery
    For each item, fb_group_id = item.groupId; if that's empty, use the part of item.url after /groups/, without slashes or query string. Skip it if this Search already has a Group with that fb_group_id (the same group can come back as www.facebook.com and web.facebook.com). Otherwise create a Group: name, url = https://www.facebook.com/groups/<fb_group_id>, privacy, members = membersCount, posts_last_30_days = postsLastMonth, posts_last_24_hours = postsLastDay, description = item.description or item.groupDescription, rules (a list of { title, description }: join each into "Title: description" lines), search_keyword, keyword_type, included = false, status "Not joined".
    Save groups_found, cost_usd = data.usageTotalUsd, run_status "done".
  - If data.status is FAILED, TIMED-OUT or ABORTED: run_status "failed", and save a short error. If the run stopped at about $0.01 with a "maximum cost per run" message, my Apify account is out of credit: stop starting new runs, set the Search status to "Failed", and set the summary to "Your Apify account is out of credit. Top up at apify.com under Billing, then press Resume search." Add a "Resume search" button that sets those failed keywords back to "queued" and calls startGroupSearch.
  - If Apify returns 401, set the summary to "Apify didn't accept your token. Check the APIFY_TOKEN secret in your Base44 settings."
- Then call startQueued(search) to use any free slots.
- total_cost_usd = sum of cost_usd.
- When no keywords are queued or running, set status "Checking groups".
- Return the Search.

FIND GROUPS PAGE BEHAVIOUR
- After "Start search", call startGroupSearch, then call checkGroupSearch every 20 seconds while the page is open, until status is "Checking groups", "Done" or "Failed".
- Show a progress list: each keyword with a status icon (waiting, searching, done with "N groups", failed), plus "Spent so far: $X.XX of your Apify credit".
- If the status is "Waiting", show "Your Apify account is busy with other runs. Your search will start automatically."
- Show: "This takes about 20–30 minutes. You can leave this page and come back; it carries on where it left off." When the page is reopened, resume checking.
- If some keywords failed but others worked, carry on with the groups that were found and say which keywords didn't work.
Step 6 of 12

Checking and ranking the groups

Filters out dead, spammy and marketer groups, then picks your top 20.

Use the cream-and-black design system from Prompt 2 for everything in this step.

When a Search reaches "Checking groups", the Find groups page shows "Checking each group…" and runs the check below, then shows a button "See my 20 groups".

To avoid timeouts, do this in steps from the page, with a progress bar: first the number rules, then the AI check in batches of 15 groups, then the ranking.

STEP A: NUMBER RULES (no AI)
- Activity bar: 300 or more posts in the last 30 days (an average of 10 a day). Use the 30-day figure, not the 24-hour one. Drop groups below 300 with drop_reason "Under 10 posts a day".
- Exception: keep the single most active group whose name contains the user's own town AND "mums", "moms" or "parents", even if it's below the bar. Set why_it_fits to start "Below 10/day, kept as your town's main parents group."
- Drop any group whose name matches the user's groups_to_avoid.

STEP B: AI CHECK, batches of 15 (InvokeLLM with a JSON schema)
Send the user's service, ideal client, not_ideal_client, country, town, region and client_reach, and for each group: id, name, description, rules, members, posts_last_30_days, keyword_type. Give the AI these rules:

- Drop groups for marketers, or run by marketers ("learn marketing strategies with us"). The admin is a competitor.
- Drop jobs, buy/sell, "for sale", "free advertising", "offers", "directory", foodie, fan, sports, council and lost-and-found groups. Keep local mums, parents and town chat groups; they're a key part of the local list.
- Drop groups for another country, or a town with the same name elsewhere (Leeds Alabama, Perth Australia, Cambridge Massachusetts, Berkshire County Massachusetts, Bracknell Tasmania).
- Local means the user's town, its county or state, or neighbouring towns within about 30 minutes. Anything further is dropped.
- Niche groups: if client_reach is "Anywhere in my country", keep only groups for that country, or clearly mostly from that country. If "Worldwide", keep English-language groups from anywhere.
- Drop niche groups that turn out to be for consumers, not business owners (e.g. a foodie group found by "restaurant owners"). Never apply this to mums or community groups.
- Advertising walls (business and niche groups only, never community groups): drop ONLY if BOTH are true: (1) the description is mostly about advertising and says nothing about advice, support, questions, networking or recommendations, AND (2) posts per day per 1,000 members is over 5 (posts_last_30_days ÷ 30 ÷ (members ÷ 1,000)). If only one is true, keep it and add "Promo-heavy, so check the feed before posting" to why_it_fits.
- Drop groups you can't join (e.g. "qualified practitioners only", "hairdressers only") unless the user would qualify.
- Drop groups whose description or rules ban marketers or service providers ("no marketing help", "social media experts will be banned"). Facebook's standard "No promotions or spam" rule is NOT a ban on marketers; almost every group has it.
- If the rules ban free-advice posts that could lead to sales, keep the group but set allows_lead_post = false and posting_rules "Comments only, no lead post".
- For mums and community groups, note how businesses can take part, e.g. "Business posts on Saturdays only" or "Recommendations thread only".
- Keep groups run by a supplier, but note "Run by <supplier>" in why_it_fits.
- If the rules clearly belong to a different topic (a restaurant group with toy-car rules), ignore them and write "Check on joining".
- Classify each kept group's type: "Niche", "Local - business" or "Local - community".

Return for every group: { id, keep (boolean), drop_reason, type, why_it_fits (one short line), posting_rules, allows_lead_post }. Save the results to each Group.

STEP C: RANK
- Niche: rank kept Niche groups by posts_last_30_days, highest first, but put allows_lead_post = false groups after the others. Take the top 10.
- Local: same ranking across Local - business and Local - community together, but aim for roughly 5 business and 5 community. Prefer groups for the user's own town over county-wide ones when activity is similar. Take the top 10.
- Set included = true and rank 1–10 for the chosen groups.
- If either list has fewer than 10, write the Search summary in plain English, e.g. "Only 6 niche groups reached 10 posts a day. Want me to search more keywords?", and show a button "Search more keywords" that goes back to keyword suggestions with the existing keywords removed. Never add groups from another country without asking.
- Set Search status to "Done".
